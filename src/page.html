How climate risk is calculated

The assessment applies the IPCC risk framework to every asset, for each hazard and period. Hazard is banded by percentile at the asset location, exposure is set by the asset sitting where the hazard occurs, and vulnerability is the ratio of sensitivity to adaptive capacity.

HazardHazard value at the asset location, banded by percentile: Low 0.4, Moderate 0.8, High 1.0.
